ElastiCache runs an **in-memory data store** for you: **Valkey** or **Redis OSS** (feature-rich, with lists, sorted sets and more) or **Memcached** (a simple key-value cache). Because everything sits in RAM, reads and writes take well under a millisecond.

The classic use is a **cache**: before asking the slow database, ask the cache. If the answer is there (a **hit**), you skip the database. If not (a **miss**), read the database and store the answer in the cache with an expiry time (**TTL**) for next time.

## Key ideas

<KeyConcepts>
  <Concept title="Cache-aside">The most common pattern: the app checks the cache, falls back to the database on a miss, then writes the result into the cache. The app is in charge, so the cache can be emptied safely at any time.</Concept>
  <Concept title="TTL">How many seconds a cached item lives before it expires. Short TTLs mean fresher data; long TTLs mean fewer database reads.</Concept>
  <Concept title="Sorted sets">A Redis and Valkey type that keeps members ordered by a score. Adding a score and reading the top 10 are both single, fast commands, which is why leaderboards love it.</Concept>
  <Concept title="Serverless or node-based">**ElastiCache Serverless** scales automatically and bills by data stored and work done. The node-based option has you choose node sizes and counts TODO(verify).</Concept>
  <Concept title="Inside your VPC">An ElastiCache cache has no public address. Your app, including any Lambda function, must be inside the same [VPC](/services/vpc) to reach it.</Concept>
</KeyConcepts>

## Where it would fit in our projects

[Quibble](/projects/quibble) does several things a cache usually does, but in DynamoDB: it saves leaderboards pre-computed in its results table, and keeps rate limits in a table of their own (`RateLimitsTable` in its template). That keeps the whole app VPC-free and pay-per-request.

At a much bigger scale, a Valkey or Redis sorted set could hold a live leaderboard (`ZINCRBY` to add points, `ZREVRANGE` for the top 10). The trade-off is a fixed monthly cost, a VPC for the Lambda functions, and one more system to keep in sync.
