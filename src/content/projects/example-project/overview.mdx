Photo Notes is a pretend app used to test this site. You type a short note, attach a photo, and the app keeps both so you can find them later.

Imagine your lab partner photographs the whiteboard. Instead of a messy chat thread, they save it as a note with the photo attached. You open the app at home and it's there.
